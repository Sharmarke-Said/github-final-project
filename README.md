# Simple Interest Calculator

A simple Bash script that calculates simple interest based on the principal amount, annual rate of interest, and time period.

## Formula

Simple Interest = P × R × T

Where:

- P = Principal amount
- R = Annual rate of interest
- T = Time period

## Usage

Run the script:

./simple-interest.sh

Enter the principal, rate of interest, and time period when prompted.

## Example

For a principal of 1000, an annual interest rate of 5%, and a time period of 2 years:

Simple Interest = 1000 × 5 × 2 / 100
Simple Interest = 100
